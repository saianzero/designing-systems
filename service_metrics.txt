SLA,SLO,SLI - RPO, RTO


SLA - Service Level Agreement
Agreement with client

(99% requests return in 5ms)

SLO - Service Level Objective
Team objectives to aim for

(99.5% requests return in 5ms)

SLI  - Service Level Indicator
Actual reality
Measures latency


RTO and RPO

Recovery Point Objective
How much data can we afford to lose?
Backup<——>Disaster

Recovery Time Objective (Accepted Tolerable downtime)
How quicky can we recover our services post disaster?

RPO, RTO inversely proportional to Costs for setting up reqd. infra


RPO <= RTO
Example
Suppose your payment database crashes at 2:00 PM.
* RPO = 15 minutes → You can tolerate losing at most 15 minutes of data.
* RTO = 1 hour → The system must be back online within 1 hour.
So:
2:00 PM failure→ Restore data from 1:45 PM or later→ System must be operational by 3:00 PM
